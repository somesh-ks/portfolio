Luminovo makes software that electronics manufacturers use to source components and quote assembly jobs. A single quote can involve hundreds of parts, dozens of suppliers and machine-time calculations that used to live in spreadsheets.

The full case study covers the sourcing redesign, the expression builder for machine-time costs, and how the team measured the outcome. It is shared under NDA.
