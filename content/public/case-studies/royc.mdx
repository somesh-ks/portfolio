ROYC builds the infrastructure banks and wealth managers use to offer private-market investments to their clients. Each bank ships ROYC under its own brand, so the product has to become someone else's product, quickly and without breaking.

The full case study covers the white-label token architecture, the investor and partner experiences, and how the design system changed the sales process. It is shared under NDA.
