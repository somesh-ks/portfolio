A gift for the Claude community in Sweden that turned into a small production line. Each sticker had to look right in Slack, in Keynote, on the web and on a laptop lid.

## Why pixel art breaks

- A pixel off by one is invisible at 2x and obvious at 8x.
- Every format has quirks. GIF has no partial transparency, Lottie wants vectors.
- Checking 40 frames by eye did not scale.

<BeforeAfterScrubber variant="stickers" />

## One drawing, every format

Each sticker is rectangles on a grid, not an image. Exporters read the model and write SVG, Lottie, GIF or a `<claude-mascot>` element. One fix fixes every format.

## Checking cells, not frames

A checker flags cells that are half-filled, off-palette or out of bounds. Its first run caught more than a week of looking.

## Looking back

- Rects make shading hard, so the style stays flat.
- Exporters took longer than drawing. It paid off by the third format.

The first print ended up on a water bottle.
