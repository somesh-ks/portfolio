The mascot has no image files. It's a grid of rectangles, and each move maps time to rectangles. Pick a move, add props, and export it still or animated.

<a className="button" href="/stickers" target="_blank" rel="noopener">
  Open studio ↗
</a>

Because it's just rects, it exports cleanly: PNG for stills (with a square version for Slack), GIF, an animated SVG that respects reduced motion, or a Lottie file for motion designers. Props like hats, shades and a mug follow the pose, and a die-cut outline turns it into a sticker.

## What I learned

- **Constraints make the style.** A blink is two rows changing colour.
- **Reduced motion is a design choice.** The mascot still changes expression. It just stops moving across the stage.
- **Sound needs consent.** Audio only starts after you turn it on.
