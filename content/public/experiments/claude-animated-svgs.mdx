The mascot has no image files. It's a grid of rectangles, and each animation maps time to rectangles. Turn on the grid to see exactly what the renderer sees.

<ClaudeMascotStudio />

Because it's just rects, it exports cleanly: an animated SVG that respects reduced motion, a Lottie file for motion designers, a framework-free `<claude-mascot>` element, or an MP4 via WebCodecs.

## What I learned

- **Constraints make the style.** A blink is two rows changing colour.
- **Reduced motion is a design choice.** The mascot still changes expression. It just stops moving across the stage.
- **Sound needs consent.** Audio only starts after you turn it on.
