Most agent interfaces show two states: waiting and done. The waiting is a spinner, and the done is a wall of text that arrives all at once. Neither tells you what the agent is doing, whether it is going the wrong way, or how to stop it.

<AgenticStreamDemo />

The right-hand side breaks the same run into steps you can read: the plan, each tool call with its arguments, and the result. You can stop it at any point. Before the agent does anything that leaves the sandbox, it asks.

## Principles

- **Show the work, not the tokens.** A step label is easier to follow than a stream of words.
- **Make stopping cheap.** The stop button is always in the same place and works instantly.
- **Ask before acting.** An approval gate turns an irreversible action into a decision a person makes.
- **Say how sure you are.** A low-confidence step looks different from a high-confidence one, in words as well as style.
