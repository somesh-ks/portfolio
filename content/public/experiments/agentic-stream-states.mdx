Most agent interfaces have two states: a spinner, then a wall of text. Neither tells you what the agent is doing or how to stop it.

<AgenticStreamDemo />

The right side splits the same run into steps: the plan, each tool call and its result. You can stop it anytime, and it asks before anything leaves the sandbox.

## Principles

- **Show the work, not the tokens.** A step label beats a stream of words.
- **Make stopping cheap.** One stop button, always in the same place.
- **Ask before acting.** An approval gate turns an irreversible action into a decision.
- **Say how sure you are.** Low confidence looks different, in words and in style.
