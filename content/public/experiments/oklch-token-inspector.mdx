HSL lies about lightness. A yellow and a blue at the same HSL lightness look nothing alike. In OKLCH, equal steps look equal, so you can generate a ramp instead of painting it.

<OklchTokenInspector />

The inspector builds ramps the way this site's tokens are built: fixed lightness per step, chroma eased towards the ends to stay in sRGB, and every pair checked against 4.5:1 for text and 3:1 for UI. Failures are named in words, not just red.

## Why it matters

A generated ramp can be regenerated. Change the brand hue and every step and contrast check follows. This site runs the same check in CI.
