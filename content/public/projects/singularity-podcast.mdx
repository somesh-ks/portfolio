Every morning Singularity collects the design engineering stories worth reading, groups them by topic and turns the briefing into audio. It started as a tool for one reader: me, on the commute, phone in one hand.

That constraint shaped the product. Every control sits within thumb reach, the player stays docked to the bottom of the screen, and a story can be skipped without looking. The interface is built on its own agent-readable design system, which became a project of its own.
