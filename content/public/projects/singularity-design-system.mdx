`@singularityfm/design-system` is the system behind the Singularity app. It is tonal and flat, sits on an 8pt grid, and treats tokens as the only allowed values: primitive, then semantic, then component.

What makes it different is the reader it was written for. An `AGENTS.md` contract tells a coding agent what it may and may not do, and `pnpm run gate` fails the build when an agent reaches for a raw hex value or an off-grid spacing. The case study [Building an Agentic Design System](/work/building-agentic-design-system) explains why.
