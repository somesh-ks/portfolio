`@singularityfm/design-system` runs the Singularity app. Tonal, flat, on an 8pt grid, and tokens are the only allowed values.

It's written for agents too. An `AGENTS.md` contract says what they may do, and `pnpm run gate` fails the build on a raw hex or off-grid spacing. [Building an Agentic Design System](/work/building-agentic-design-system) explains why.
