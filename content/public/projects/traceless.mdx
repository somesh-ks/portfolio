Personal data in India travels through a long chain of apps, brokers and agencies, and most families have no way to see where theirs ended up. Traceless helps one household find out and ask for it to be removed.

It runs on Cloudflare Workers with Hono, stores state in D1, KV and R2, and fails closed: every request needs a valid Access JWT, and nothing that touches a third party leaves the system without a person approving it in a queue first. Automation drafts the request. A human sends it.
