Coding agents are good at producing interfaces that work. They are less good at producing interfaces that feel right, because the rules that make software feel right live in people's heads: why a dropdown should open in 180ms and not 400ms, why a popover scales from its trigger, why a hover effect has no business on a touch screen.

These skills write those rules down in a form an agent can follow. Each skill owns one topic, such as tokens, color, typography, motion or accessibility review, and says when to use it and when to hand off to another skill. Values live in one file, `DESIGN.md`, so a change to a duration or a contrast ratio happens once.

This portfolio was built with them.
